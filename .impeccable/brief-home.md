# Home — Byond Intima

Scope: home page (`/`) plus the shared site shell (nav, footer, mobile nav). Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: Hebrew-speaking couples; some arrive to buy a gift. Action: join the subscription ("the experience"), or buy one of the three boxes. Proof: real box names, contents, durations and prices only; subscription price, season length and discount % are not defined yet and stay visibly unfilled. No ratings or reviews.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The subscription is a season programme. Each couple evening is a numbered screening on a printed programme, and the couple's space is their membership card. Refuses the category default of a black-and-gold luxury hero with a centred headline over three equal product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Programme newsprint ground (#F1ECE2), one black ink (#16130F), one season spot colour that changes per season (season 01: vermilion #E0442A), and a dark screening-room tone (#1C1A1E) reserved for counters and the member card. Condensed Hebrew display numerals and headings, a plain Hebrew grotesk for body. Heavy 3px ink rules, schedule tables, ticket stubs with dashed perforations. No gold, no gradients, no rounded cards, no shadows.

STORY: The visitor sees that Byond Intima is a season of guided couple evenings, understands that subscribing brings the seasons and a member discount, sees the three boxes as the season's headline screenings (דמדומים, חצות, עד אור הבוקר — dusk to dawn), and either joins or buys one box.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead bar with numbered nav (01 המנוי, 02 המארזים, 03 הבוטיק, 04 המקום שלכם) and a member-card link. Left column in RTL order: the cover — kicker, a giant spot-coloured season number "01" over "עונה של ערבים שלכם", one-paragraph lede, and the primary action as a ticket with a perforated stub reading "להצטרף למנוי"; a secondary text link to single boxes. Right column: the season programme table — numbered evenings with title, theme and whether it is a box or included; below it the fixed-digit counters (member discount, days to next season) shown as unfilled until real numbers exist.

FORM: Season programme / cinematheque subscription booklet; own-list position 3 of 7 (assigned); seed:c1bfa6157b216dbc. Signature interaction: the ticket stub tears off along the perforation when the visitor joins or adds a box to the cart. Motion grammar: printed, mechanical, short — rows set in like type, stubs tear, digits flip; nothing floats or glows.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
